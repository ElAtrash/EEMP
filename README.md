# EEMP - Energy Efficiency Management Platform for Tourism

**Empowering Azorean Tourism Properties to Achieve Miosótis Certification and Reduce Energy Costs**

---

## Executive Summary

A SaaS platform designed specifically for Azorean tourism properties (hotels, guesthouses, rural tourism, local accommodation) to track energy consumption, manage sustainability compliance, and achieve official Miosótis Azores certification—the region's government-backed sustainability standard.

**Target Launch:** Q2 2026
**Primary Market:** Azores (9 islands, ~1,400+ tourism properties)
**Future Expansion:** Mainland Portugal, other Atlantic islands

---

## The Problem We're Solving

### **1. Azorean Tourism Properties Face Critical Sustainability Challenges**

**Government Pressure:**

- Azores is the world's first archipelago certified as an EarthCheck Sustainable Destination (2019)
- Regional government has established Miosótis Azores certification as the official standard
- Properties MUST demonstrate sustainability to remain competitive
- Increasing regulatory requirements for energy reporting and environmental compliance

**Economic Pain Points:**

- Energy costs represent 6-7% of operating budget (€2,200+ per room annually for hotels)
- Small properties (5-20 rooms) cannot afford enterprise energy management systems (€10,000+ annually)
- Manual tracking via spreadsheets is time-consuming, error-prone, and insufficient for certification
- Properties lose competitive advantage without eco-labels (52% of travelers notice and value sustainability)

**Market Context:**

- **4.5 million** overnight stays in 2025 (record growth despite late-year slowdown)
- **48.6%** of stays now in local accommodation vs 45.9% in hotels (local accommodation growing fastest)
- Tourism is booming but traditional hotels face pressure from agile, eco-conscious guesthouses
- Only **136 properties currently Miosótis certified** (massive untapped market)

### **2. Existing Solutions Don't Serve This Market**

**Enterprise Platforms:**

- Cost €10,000-50,000+ annually (prohibitive for small properties)
- Designed for large hotel chains, not 5-20 room guesthouses
- No integration with Miosótis Azores certification requirements
- No Portuguese language or local support
- Require IoT hardware investment (€5,000-20,000)

**Current Reality for Small Properties:**

- Using Excel/Google Sheets to track utility bills
- Manual calculation of per-room consumption
- Paper documentation for certifications
- No centralized system to prepare for audits
- Cannot demonstrate sustainability to booking platforms or conscious travelers

### **3. The Certification Gap**

**Miosótis Azores Facts:**

- Government-backed, **FREE certification** for tourism properties
- Covers Energy Management, Water Management, Waste Management
- **Only 136 out of 1,400+** tourism properties are certified
- Properties need systematic tracking to qualify and maintain certification
- Certification expires every 2 years (requires continuous monitoring)

**Barriers to Certification:**

- Lack of affordable tools to track required metrics
- Confusion about documentation requirements
- Time-consuming manual process
- No guidance on how to improve compliance scores

## Our Solution

### **What We're Building**

A **lightweight, affordable, Azores-focused SaaS platform** that enables small tourism properties to:

1. **Track Energy & Utilities** - Simple dashboard for electricity, water, gas, waste, and renewable energy consumption
2. **Achieve Miosótis Certification** - Step-by-step guided checklist with evidence management and automated reporting
3. **Reduce Costs** - Identify energy waste, benchmark against similar properties, calculate ROI on efficiency investments
4. **Market Sustainability** - Generate professional reports for booking platforms, guests, and grant applications

### **How It Works**

**For Property Owners:**

1. Sign up and add property details (rooms, location, type)
2. Input monthly utility readings (from bills or auto-import)
3. View instant dashboard: consumption trends, per-room metrics, carbon footprint
4. Work through Miosótis certification checklist (auto-populated with tracked data)
5. Upload evidence documents (policies, training records, certificates)
6. Generate certification reports for government auditors
7. Export sustainability reports for marketing and compliance

**Technology Approach:**

- **MVP:** Manual data entry (no hardware required)
- **Phase 2:** API integration with Azorean utility providers (EDA, Águas dos Açores)
- **Phase 3:** IoT sensor integration for real-time monitoring (optional premium tier)

## Key Certifications This Platform Supports

### **Primary: Miosótis Azores (2023 GSTC-Recognized)**

**Why This Is Our Strategic Focus:**

1. **Government-Backed** - Official Regional Government of Azores program
2. **Free for Properties** - No certification fees (removes barrier to entry)
3. **GSTC-Recognized** - Upgraded in 2023 to meet Global Sustainable Tourism Council criteria
4. **Local Relevance** - Specifically designed for Azorean tourism context
5. **Marketing Value** - Helps tourists find certified green accommodations
6. **Partnership Potential** - Could become "official" digital tool for Regional Secretariat for Environment

**Miosótis Requirements Our Platform Tracks:**

- Energy consumption monitoring (electricity, gas, renewables)
- Water usage tracking and conservation
- Waste management and recycling documentation
- GHG emissions calculations
- Sustainable management practices documentation
- Socioeconomic and cultural impact tracking

**Current Stats:**

- 136 properties certified (out of 1,400+)
- Free program with technical monitoring provided
- Based on 4 GSTC pillars: Sustainable Management, Socioeconomic, Cultural, Environmental

### **Secondary: International Certifications (Premium Tier)**

**Travelife Gold:**

- 161 sustainability indicators
- Recognized by major tour operators
- €3,000-5,000 audit cost
- Renewed every 2 years
- 7 Azorean hotels currently certified (Bensaude Hotels Collection)

**EarthCheck:**

- The certification used by Azores as a region
- 450+ data points
- Science-based benchmarking
- 4 levels: Silver → Gold → Platinum → Master (over 15 years)
- Annual audits required

**Green Key:**

- Simpler, entry-level certification
- 13 criteria areas
- Good for small properties starting sustainability journey

### **Tertiary: Regulatory Compliance Support**

**EU CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive):**

- For larger properties (>250 employees or €50M revenue)
- ESG reporting requirements
- Our platform provides necessary data exports

**Portugal 2030 Grant Reporting:**

- Properties applying for sustainability grants need proof of energy tracking
- Our platform generates required documentation

## Technical Architecture (High-Level)

### **Stack**

- **Backend:** Ruby on Rails 8
- **Frontend:** React + Inertia.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (with time-series optimization)
- **Storage:** Active Storage (evidence files)
- **Deployment:** Fly.io or Heroku (European region)
- **Authentication:** Rails built-in + Google OAuth

### **Key Models**

- Property (hotels, guesthouses, etc.)
- UtilityReading (monthly consumption data)
- MonthlySummary (calculated metrics)
- MiosotisChecklistItem (certification requirements)
- Evidence (uploaded documents)

### **Services**

- MonthlySummaryCalculator (compute per-room metrics)
- CarbonCalculator (emissions from consumption)
- MiosotisComplianceCalculator (certification score)
- ComparisonService (year-over-year analysis)

### **Security & Compliance**

- GDPR-compliant data handling
- Data encryption at rest and in transit
- Multi-tenant architecture (data isolation)
- Regular backups (hourly snapshots)
- EU-based hosting (data sovereignty)
